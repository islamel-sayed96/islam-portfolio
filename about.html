About me

The story behind the work

Hello there! I'm Islam El-sayed, a full-stack developer and digital marketer graduated from the Information Technology Institute (ITI), originally trained on the ASP.NET stack (C#, MSSQL, LINQ & Entity Framework, WinForms, ASP.NET MVC / Core / Web API) with a solid grounding in object-oriented programming.

From there I branched into modern front-end engineering — React, Angular, TypeScript — and UI/UX design, taking products from wireframe through to a polished, user-tested interface in Figma.

Today my work sits at the intersection of building and growing: I design and ship full web products end to end, and I run the digital marketing side too — SEO, paid ad campaigns, social media management, and CRM/operations — for real estate, education, and beauty-industry clients across the UAE.

Alongside client work, I teach — software engineering, web design, e-commerce, and digital marketing — at multiple institutes in Dubai, mentoring the next generation of developers and entrepreneurs through hands-on, practical training.

I consider myself a fast learner, a team player, a curious and disciplined person — always comfortable moving between the technical and the strategic side of a project.
